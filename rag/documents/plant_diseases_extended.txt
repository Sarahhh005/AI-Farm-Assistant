Crop: Apple | Disease: Apple Scab (Apple___Apple_scab)
Symptoms:
- Olive-green to brown lesions appear on leaves.
- Infected leaves may develop spots and drop prematurely.
- Dark scabby lesions can form on fruit.
Management:
- Remove and dispose of infected plant material.
- Improve air circulation around the tree.
- Follow locally recommended fungicide practices when necessary.
Prevention:
- Maintain good orchard sanitation.
- Remove fallen infected leaves and fruit.
- Improve canopy airflow and avoid prolonged leaf wetness.

Crop: Apple | Disease: Cedar Apple Rust (Apple___Cedar_apple_rust)Crop: Apple | Disease: Cedar Apple Rust (Apple___Cedar_apple_rust)
Symptoms: Bright yellow-orange spots on upper leaf surfaces, later with tiny black dots; small tube-like projections underneath; raised lesions on fruit.
Management: Remove nearby juniper or red cedar galls where practical; time protectant fungicides from pink bud to petal fall per local advice; remove badly infected leaves.
Prevention: Plant resistant varieties; keep apples away from junipers; scout during wet spring weather.

Crop: Cherry | Disease: Powdery Mildew (Cherry_(including_sour)___Powdery_mildew)
Symptoms: White powdery fungal growth on leaves and shoots; leaves curl, distort or turn pale; fruit can be affected in severe cases.
Management: Prune out infected shoots; improve airflow and light in the canopy; apply fungicide only per local recommendations when disease appears early.

Crop: Grape | Disease: Black Rot (Grape___Black_rot)
Symptoms: Small brown circular leaf spots with dark borders and tiny black dots; berries rot and shrivel into hard black mummies.
Management: Remove mummified berries and infected tissue; follow a protectant fungicide program from early shoot growth through fruit set per local advice; open the canopy.
Prevention: Vineyard sanitation; canopy pruning; train vines for fast drying.

Crop: Grape | Disease: Esca / Black Measles (Grape___Esca_(Black_Measles))
Symptoms: Tiger-stripe pattern of yellow or red interveinal leaf tissue; dark-spotted berries; sudden vine collapse; internal wood decay.
Management: No complete cure; remove and destroy dead or affected wood; prune in dry weather and protect wounds; replace severely affected vines.
Prevention: Use clean planting material; limit large pruning wounds; avoid pruning in wet periods; sanitize tools.

Crop: Grape | Disease: Leaf Blight / Isariopsis Leaf Spot (Grape___Leaf_blight_(Isariopsis_Leaf_Spot))
